Use your phone as the camera

A phone camera is usually sharper than a laptop's, which also helps the AI. Once connected, the phone appears in the Camera list like any webcam.

iPhone (cable)

  1. Install Camo on the iPhone and Camo Studio on this computer. The free version is 720p.
  2. Install iTunes on this computer so Windows can talk to the iPhone over the cable.
  3. Plug the iPhone in, tap Trust, and open Camo on both.
  4. Pick Camo in the Camera list here.

Android (cable)

  1. Android 14 or newer: plug in, pull down the notification for USB, and choose Webcam. Not every phone maker turned this on.
  2. If there is no Webcam option, use DroidCam (phone app plus Windows client) instead.
  3. Pick the phone in the Camera list here.
